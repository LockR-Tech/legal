Privacy policy

Version 03/11/2026 · Applies to the Lock.R app, the Lock.R website and the kiosks at our lockers.

This policy explains what personal data Lock.R ("we") collects, what we use it for, who we share it with and what rights you have. It goes together with the Terms of service.

1. Data we collect

  • Account: name, phone number, email, profile photo, password (stored only as a bcrypt hash).
  • Sign-in with Google, Facebook or phone number: display name, email, public profile photo and the identifier issued by the provider. We never receive your social account password.
  • Orders: lockers and boxes used, times, order codes, status, parcel weight, drone flight path, payment and refund history.
  • Recipients and delegates: name, phone number and email entered by the sender so we can deliver the pickup code. Senders must have their consent before providing them.
  • Payments: amount, reference code and result of the bank transfer. We do not store card numbers or bank login details.
  • Location: when you allow it, to find lockers near you, give directions and tag incident photos. For technicians, location is only collected during their shift.
  • Camera, photos and Bluetooth: only when you use the related feature: scanning QR codes, taking incident or profile photos, opening a nearby box over Bluetooth.
  • Assistant: your questions and the answers; when you ask about orders, the details of orders that involve you.
  • Technical: push notification token (FCM), app language, error logs and server access logs.

2. How we use it

  • Create, authenticate and protect your account.
  • Provide the service: locker rental, sending and receiving parcels, drone delivery, delegation, sending box codes.
  • Handle payments, refunds, incidents and compensation.
  • Send notifications about orders, pickup codes, deadlines and account changes.
  • Support you and answer questions through the assistant.
  • Detect fraud, keep lockers, drones and people safe, and comply with the law.

We do not use your data for advertising and we never sell it.

3. Third parties that receive data

We share only the data each provider needs:

  • Google Firebase, Google and Facebook: sign-in (phone number, Google, Facebook) and push notifications.
  • SePay and banks: reconciling VietQR bank transfers.
  • Twilio and email services: sending box codes, OTP codes and order details to phone numbers and email addresses.
  • Cloudinary: storing profile and incident photos.
  • Anthropic (Claude) and Voyage AI: processing questions sent to the assistant.
  • OpenStreetMap and OSRM: showing maps and calculating routes; they only receive the coordinates needed to draw the map.
  • Microsoft Azure and Cloudflare: hosting and running our systems.

We also provide data to competent authorities when the law requires it.

4. Where data is stored and how it is protected

  • Data is stored on Microsoft Azure servers in Malaysia, which is a transfer of data outside Vietnam under Vietnamese personal data protection law.
  • Connections are encrypted with HTTPS; access to data is limited by role; administrators sign in with two-factor authentication.
  • No system is perfectly secure. If we discover a data breach, we handle it and notify you and the authorities as the law requires.

5. How long we keep it

  • Account data is kept while your account is active.
  • Order and transaction data is kept for as long as accounting and tax law requires, even after you delete your account; it is anonymised where possible.
  • Incident photos, technical logs and assistant conversations are deleted when no longer needed for the purposes above.

6. Your rights

  • View and edit your profile in the app.
  • Withdraw location, camera, Bluetooth or notification permissions in your phone settings at any time.
  • Ask for a copy of your data, or ask us to restrict, stop or delete processing.
  • Withdraw your consent. Features that depend on that data will then stop working for you.
  • Complain to the competent authority if you believe your rights have been infringed.

To request deletion of your account and data, see the Data deletion guide. We respond within 30 days.

7. Children

The service is not for people under 16 without the consent of a parent or guardian. If we learn we have collected a child's data without that consent, we delete it.

8. Changes to this policy

When this policy changes we update the version at the top and let you know in the app. Significant changes require you to agree again.
